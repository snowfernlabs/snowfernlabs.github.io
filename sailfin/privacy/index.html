Privacy policy: Sailfin

Last updated 2026-11-22.

Sailfin is a fishing log that keeps your data on your phone.

What the app stores

  • Your catches, notes, photos, tackle list, water names and settings.
  • If you turn on Save location with each catch: the position where each catch was logged.

Sailfin stores your fishing log on your device. There is no Sailfin account, no Sailfin server, no analytics and no advertising.

What leaves your phone

  • Weather lookups. When location is on, the app sends a position rounded to about 100 m to Open-Meteo to fetch weather. The request includes the date range needed for the lookup. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy applies to that request.
  • Map tiles. When you tap Open online map in Insights, Sailfin requests map images for the area you view from OpenStreetMap. The individual catch points and heat layer are drawn on your device. The map can show previously recorded catches even if you have turned off location logging for new catches.
  • Tides and river flow. Sailfin chooses nearby NOAA Tides & Currents stations on the device and requests predictions and readings by station ID. The USGS Water Data API receives a bounding box based on a rounded position and the time window for river flow or freshwater temperature readings.
  • Speaking a catch. "Record a catch" (and the widget, tile and shortcut) opens your phone's own speech-to-text service (Google's on most phones). The app asks for on-device recognition, but the service may process your voice online under its own privacy policy; the app only receives the text. Typing a catch avoids it.
  • Google Play reviews. Sailfin may show Google's optional rating and review prompt. If you submit a rating or review, Google Play receives it. You can manage or delete your reviews through Google Play.
  • Backups and exports are files you choose to save or share. A cloud storage provider or recipient you select can receive those files. Automatic backup writes to the folder you choose.
  • Android backup. If you use Android cloud backup, the log and settings can be included. Photos are included in Sailfin ZIP backups and supported Android device-to-device transfers.

Permissions

  • Location (optional): only to record where and in what weather you caught a fish. Asked only when you turn the feature on.
  • Internet: used for the weather, tide, river and map-tile requests above.
  • The camera and photo picker are opened through Android's own screens. The app doesn't ask for camera or storage permission.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Sailfin removes its local app data. Backups may remain in Android backup or wherever you saved them. Manage those copies through Android or the storage service you used.

Children

Sailfin is intended for people aged 13 and older. It has no Sailfin account. The optional services described above handle their own data.
