Privacy policy: Sailfin

Last updated 2026-11-18.

Sailfin is a fishing log that keeps your data on your phone.

What the app stores

  • Your catches, notes, photos, tackle list, water names and settings.
  • If you turn on Save location with each catch: the position where each catch was logged.

All of this is stored only on your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

What leaves your phone

  • Weather lookups. When location is on, the app sends a position rounded to about 100 m to Open-Meteo to fetch the weather for a catch. No identifier or other data is sent with it. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy applies to that request.
  • Map tiles. The "Where you catch" map in Insights loads map images from OpenStreetMap's tile servers, which see which area is being viewed (not your catch points; the heat layer is drawn on the phone). The map only loads when you open that screen and have catches with a location.
  • Tides and river flow. When location is on, the Today screen also sends a position rounded to about 1 km to NOAA's Tides & Currents service (nearest tide station and its predictions) and the USGS Water Services (nearest stream gauge). No identifier or other data is sent with it.
  • Speaking a catch. "Record a catch" (and the widget, tile and shortcut) opens your phone's own speech-to-text service (Google's on most phones). The app asks for on-device recognition, but the service may process your voice online under its own privacy policy; the app only receives the text. Typing a catch avoids it.
  • Backups and exports are files you choose to save or share. The app doesn't upload them anywhere.
  • Android backup. If you use Android's own device backup, your log is included in it, like other app data.

Permissions

  • Location (optional): only to record where and in what weather you caught a fish. Asked only when you turn the feature on.
  • Internet: only for the weather, tide, river and map-tile requests above.
  • The camera and photo picker are opened through Android's own screens. The app doesn't ask for camera or storage permission.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Backup files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It has no account and the developer receives none of your data; the only data that leaves the phone is described under "What leaves your phone".
