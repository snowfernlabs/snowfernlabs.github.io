Sailfin support

Do I need an account?

No. Your catch log is stored on your device.

Can I use Sailfin offline?

You can log catches and browse the bundled field guide offline. Live weather, water conditions, and map tiles need a connection.

How do I move my log to another phone?

In Sailfin, use Settings → Back up to save a copy of your log. Use Settings → Restore on the new device to load that file. Restoring replaces the current log on that device.
